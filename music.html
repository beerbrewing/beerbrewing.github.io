Pyrmont Brewery Music Room

Pyrmont Brewery Music Room is pioneering the future of music production with WebXR Augmented Reality (AR) experiences. Our platform enables musicians and producers to control musical plugins and devices in immersive AR environments, including hand gesture control for live MIDI manipulation.

  • WebXR AR tools for interactive music production
  • Hand gesture control for musical plugins and MIDI devices
  • Ableton Live SDK for creating Max4Live devices in Rust
  • Manipulate live MIDI streams in real time
  • Innovative, open source, and developer-friendly

Augmented Reality for Musicians

Experience music production in a new dimension. Use AR to:

  • Control effects and instruments with hand gestures
  • Visualize and interact with MIDI data in real time
  • Collaborate in immersive environments

Rust SDK for Max4Live

Build custom Max4Live devices for Ableton Live using the Rust programming language. Achieve high performance, reliability, and new creative possibilities for live MIDI manipulation.

  • Prototype new MIDI effects and AR-enabled instruments
  • Integrate with Ableton Live for seamless workflows
  • Expand your music production toolkit
